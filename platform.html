SECTION 5

Simplifying data analysis with intuitive visualizations and live dashboards so that you can easily uncover insights and find powerful stories to share

Analytics and dashboards are integral components of the Unomer platform to simplify the market research and data analysis processes, providing intuitive visualizations to support decision-making.

Live dashboards can be used to track survey responses and consumer data trends. Integrated tools enable data analysis functions filters, drill-downs, data cross-tabulation, significance testing and data export.
