ABOUT US

Unomer provides technology and data solutions for consumer insights that enable brands to succeed

The pace of digitization is relentless. Technologies like mobile, AI, video and smart devices are seamlessly integrated into every facet of our lives. We are inspired by the amazing possibilities of using data to better understand consumers, build products that matter, and communicate what truly resonates.

We provide consumer insights, market research, and data intelligence solutions that help brands to succeed. Our technology delivers insights with speed, scale, and precision, empowering clients to make marketing and product decisions backed by reliable data.

Our platform combines extensive access to consumers and data with sharp targeting, effective engagement, AI-driven analytics, and intuitive dashboards. This enables us to deliver accurate, comprehensive, and actionable insights enabling clients to win in their markets.
