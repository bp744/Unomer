OVERVIEW

How Barrows developed an impressive new business pitch

Titan, a leading player in the luxury retail and accessories industry, holds a prestigious position in India's marketplace. Renowned for its wide range of products, Titan operates across various categories including exquisite watches, stylish belts, premium wallets, and elegant wall clocks.

Notably, Titan has successfully ventured into the international brand license division, securing the exclusive rights to distribute and market internationally acclaimed brands such as Tommy Hilfiger, Kenneth Cole, and Anne Klein within India.

This business division positions Titan as a prominent purveyor of luxury and sophistication, catering to the discerning tastes of its clientele. They have an exclusive team to own the marketing initiatives for Titan’s licensed brands.

BRAND CHALLENGE

The premium variant launch was creating unexpected market dynamics

The Core Problem

Colgate India had recently launched a premium toothpaste variant — Colgate Charcoal Clean — with significant above-the-line media support. Within two months of launch, their internal sales data showed an unexpected dip in sales velocity for their flagship product, Colgate Strong Teeth. The Consumer Insights team suspected cannibalization but lacked fast, reliable data to confirm this hypothesis before the next quarterly media planning cycle.

The traditional research agency model would have taken 8–10 weeks and ₹40–50 lakh for a multi-city tracker. With the media plan due in 6 weeks, the team needed an answer in days — not months. They also needed the data cut by city, SEC classification, and purchase frequency to understand whether it was genuinely new buyers switching up, or loyal users migrating within the portfolio.
